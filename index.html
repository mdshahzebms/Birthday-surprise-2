Happy Birthday, Meri Sabse Khaas Insaan ❤️

Pata hai, aaj ka din mere liye kitna khaas hai? Kyunki aaj us insaan ka janamdin hai jise sochte hi mere chehre par muskurahat aa jaati hai. ❤️ Aap meri zindagi 🌎 ka wo hissa ho jise main lafzon mein poori tarah bayan nahi kar sakta. Aapki ek chhoti si baat mera din bana deti hai, aur aapki ek muskurahat mere dil ko sukoon de deti hai.

Shayad aapko andaza bhi nahi hoga ki aap meri zindagi mein kitni ahmiyat rakhti ho. ❤️ Aaj aapke birthday par main bas itna kehna chahta hoon ki aap meri har dua mein shamil ho. Main dil se chahta hoon ki aapki zindagi mein kabhi kisi cheez ki kami na ho. Aapke har sapne poore hon, har manzil aapko mile aur 🌹 aap hamesha khush raho.

Aapke saath baat karna, aapki fikr karna aur aapko khush dekhna mujhe 🦋 sach mein bahut pasand hai. Kabhi kabhi lagta hai ki meri aadat ban gayi ho aap. Din kitna bhi busy ho, ek baar nahi har baar har baat mein aapka khayal zaroor aa hi jaata hai. ❤️

Aaj ke din main Allah se bas yehi dua karta hoon ki aapki zindagi hamesha khushiyon se bhari rahe. Aapke chehre ki muskurahat kabhi kam na ho aur 🌹❤️ jis cheez ko dil se chaho wo aapko zaroor mile.

Aur haan, bas aaj aakhri baar thank you bolne do yrr, pakka fir kabhi nahi bolunga ☺️ Thank you, thank you so much 😘 ... meri zindagi mein aane ke liye, meri baaton ko sunne 🐼 ke liye, aur bina kuch kahe bhi mujhe samajh lene ke liye. Aap sach mein mere liye bahut special ho.

Janamdin mubarak ho meri jaan. ❤️ Aaj ka din aapke naam, aur meri har dua bhi aapke naam.

Khush raho, muskurate raho aur hamesha mere dil ke sabse kareeb raho. ❤️ I love you more than words can ever explain. ❤️🫂🕊️
